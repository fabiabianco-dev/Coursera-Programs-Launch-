## Why this has to go before the all-employee email

The timeline sequences manager announcements ahead of the all-employee send for one
reason: managers get asked about this within an hour of the company email landing. If they
hear about it at the same time as their teams, they cannot answer, and the launch reads as
something done to the organization rather than with it.

`project.md` states the rule as load-bearing: **manager comms → all-employee email →
webinar invites → webinars → program starts.**
